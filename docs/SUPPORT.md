# Support — Luminore

Luminore is a free, open-source board game app for iPhone and iPad. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with any other board game or its publisher.

## Get help or report a problem

Open an issue: <https://github.com/JunxuanB/Luminore/issues>. Please include your device model, iOS version, the play mode (Local Network, Internet or Nearby) and what happened.

## Common questions

**I can't see my friend's room (Local Network / Nearby).**
Both devices need Wi‑Fi on and, for Local Network, to be on the same network. Check Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network and make sure Luminore is allowed. For Nearby, keep Bluetooth on and stay close together.

**How do I play online?**
Choose Internet, create a room and share the 6‑character room code. Your friend chooses Internet › Join Room and enters the code.

**Can I play alone?**
Yes. Create a room in any mode, tap Add AI Player, then Configure Game. The Tutorial also plays entirely offline.

**How do I change the language?**
Tap the profile icon on the home screen › Language. English and Simplified Chinese are available.

**Where are my medals and match history?**
On your device and in your own private iCloud. They appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account.

**Is it free?**
The standard game over a local connection is free forever. As the player base grows, non-standard modes and online play may carry a small fee to cover server and maintenance costs; the app will say so clearly before anything changes.
